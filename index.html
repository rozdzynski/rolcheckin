How reviews are scored
Evaluation will be scored on a 5-point scale, 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
Scores with decimals sit between levels: 4.23 counts as "Doing more", working toward 5.
How earned time off works
For every 40 classes you teach, you earn 1 paid class. Your own classes and the ones you cover for a teammate both count.
  • It adds up as you go: 87 classes = 2.18 paid classes.
  • It's paid out at the end of the year.
  • The count starts over every January 1. Past years stay in your history.
Why we do this: showing up week after week is what makes RŌL great. This is our thank-you: the more you teach, the more you earn.
